Every failure from the guide. Fixed by the formula.

Reason 1: Cotton Has No Memory — Solved

The 2% elastane in every sock provides continuous elastic recovery.

Every step stretches the sock slightly. The elastane pulls it back.

Reason 2: Your Foot in a Wet Sponge — Solved

The 20% polyamide changes the moisture behavior of the entire blend.

Polyamide wicks. Mixed with cotton at this ratio, moisture moves away from the skin instead of staying in the fiber.

The foot that felt damp at 3pm in a cotton sock feels normal in a 78/20 sock.

Reason 3: One Material, Three Zones — Solved

The polyamide is concentrated at the heel and toe.

Reason 4: The Wash Sensitivity Problem — Solved

Reason 5: Surface Degradation — Solved

Combed cotton has no short fibers to work loose.

The surface on day one is the surface on wash 50.

Metafields & Reviews — Quick Answers

You asked two things on May 10 — which app to use for metafields, and which reviews app + how we treat real vs imported reviews. Here's the short version of both.

1 · Metafields

Our pick Matrixify — but try the Shopify API first

Our go-to app is Matrixify (formerly Excelify) for bulk import/export of metafields via CSV. Alfred has used it extensively across our client work — he's a master with it, so if you or Nadja get stuck, you can ask him directly.

Worth trying first

Before subscribing to anything, I'd have Nadja try doing it through the Shopify Admin API with Claude Code first. Most metafield work — creating definitions, bulk-updating values across products, syncing from a sheet — is straightforward through the API and doesn't need a paid app. You'd save the subscription cost.

If you let me know what specifically you're trying to accomplish with the metafields (the actual job — pricing tiers per variant, custom product specs, fulfillment data, sizing, etc.), I can test it on my end against the Shopify API and confirm whether it's doable without an app. If it's not, Matrixify is the answer.

2 · Reviews

Our pick Judge.me + post-purchase email from day one

We've tested all the major ones over the years. Judge.me is our default — by far the most cost-effective and scales fine. The others have stronger marketing but the subscription cost is hard to justify against margin, and the actual review-collection mechanics aren't meaningfully better.

Real vs imported reviews

Strong bias toward real custom reviews. They're more trustworthy on-page, they don't fade in credibility, and they compound as you collect more. Turn on Judge.me's post-purchase email day one — that's the engine.

About importing Amazon reviews onto Shopify

Had my agent look into this for you and the short answer is don't. Amazon's Conditions of Use prohibit copying or republishing review content for commercial use on another site, and the reviewer (not Amazon, not you) owns the copyright on what they wrote. That's why Judge.me itself refuses to build a native Amazon importer.

Keep things clean and skip the Amazon-to-Shopify review pull.
